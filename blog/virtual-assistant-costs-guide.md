## Virtual Assistant Hourly Rates in 2026

### Entry-Level VAs (€8-€15/hour)
**Perfect for:** Administrative tasks, email management, data entry, scheduling

**Common Tasks:**
- Inbox management and email filtering
- Calendar scheduling and appointment booking
- Data entry and spreadsheet updates
- Basic customer service responses
- Social media posting (pre-approved content)

**Best For:** Startups and solopreneurs needing basic administrative support

---

### Mid-Level VAs (€15-€25/hour)
**Perfect for:** Project coordination, content management, light bookkeeping

**Common Tasks:**
- All entry-level tasks PLUS:
- CRM management (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.)
- Basic bookkeeping and invoicing
- Content scheduling across platforms
- Customer onboarding
- Light graphic design (Canva)
- Research and competitive analysis

**Best For:** Growing businesses with 2-5 team members

---

### Advanced VAs (€25-€45/hour)
**Perfect for:** Specialized skills, project management, tech setup

**Common Tasks:**
- All mid-level tasks PLUS:
- Website management (WordPress, Shopify)
- Advanced spreadsheet modeling
- Email automation setup (Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign)
- Project management (Asana, ClickUp)
- Basic video editing
- Lead generation and outreach

**Best For:** Established businesses scaling operations

## How to Hire Your First Virtual Assistant

### Step 1: Identify Your Pain Points
List the tasks that:
- Drain your energy
- Could be done by anyone with training
- You'd rather not do at all
- Are repetitive and time-consuming

### Step 2: Define Clear Outcomes
Instead of "manage email," say:
- "Reduce inbox to zero by 5 PM daily"
- "Respond to customer inquiries within 2 hours"
- "Flag urgent emails for my review"

### Step 3: Choose Your Working Model
- **Hourly** (flexible, pay only for what you use)
- **Weekly Retainer** (20 hours/week at discounted rate)
- **Project-Based** (fixed scope, fixed price)

### Step 4: Where to Find VAs
- **Upwork/Fiverr:** Good for one-off tasks
- **LinkedIn:** Professional candidates
- **VA Agencies:** Higher quality, higher cost
