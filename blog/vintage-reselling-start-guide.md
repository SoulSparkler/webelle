## The €0 Startup Method: Consignment First

### How It Works:
Instead of buying inventory upfront, you:
1. Partner with local vintage collectors
2. Photograph their pieces
3. List them on eBay, Depop, or Instagram
4. Ship ONLY when something sells
5. Split profits 50/50 (or negotiate better over time)

### Why This Beats Buying Inventory:
✅ **Zero financial risk** – You never spend money on unsold items  
✅ **Learn the market** – Understand what sells before investing  
✅ **Build customer base** – Collect emails from buyers  
✅ **Negotiate better terms** – Show sellers you deliver sales

## Step-by-Step: Launching Your Vintage Business

### Phase 1: Preparation (Week 1)

**Day 1-2: Define Your Niche**
Don't try to sell "vintage." Specialize:
- **90s streetwear** (Nike, Tommy Hilfiger, Fila)
- **Designer vintage** (Chanel, Gucci, vintage Levis)
- **Boho/70s** (flowy dresses, embroidery, suede)
- **Workwear** (vintage Dickies, Carhartt, uniforms)
- **Luxury bags** (authentic vintage designer)

**Your niche = less competition + higher margins + easier marketing**

**Day 3-4: Build Your Portfolio**
- Use your phone (iPhone cameras are amazing)
- Natural light only (window shot, no flash)
- Flat lay + on-model + detail shots
- Note every measurement, fabric, flaw

**Day 5-7: Choose Your Platform**
**Best for Beginners:**
- **Depop** – Gen Z fashion, built-in vintage audience
- **eBay** – Massive reach, vintage buyers already searching
- **Instagram** – Build brand, then drive to shop
- **Vinted** – Europe's fastest-growing (great for EU market)

**Pro Tip:** Start with ONE platform, master it, then expand.

---

### Phase 2: Sourcing Inventory (Week 2-3)

**Option A: Consignment Partnerships (€0 investment)**
- Approach local vintage collectors
- Say: "I'll sell your vintage for you, you keep 50%, I keep 50%"
- No risk for them, easy money for you
- Start with friends/family's unused vintage

**Option B: Thrift Flipping (€50-100 investment)**
- Visit thrift stores 2-3x weekly
- Look for: Brand names, quality fabric, unique details
- Buy low (€2-10), sell high (€30-150)
- Clean/repair before selling

**Option C: Estate Sales & Flea Markets**
- Weekend hunts = best finds
- Go early for first access
- Bring cash for better deals
- Build relationships with vendors

---

### Phase 3: Photography & Listings (Week 3-4)

**Photography Checklist:**
☑ Natural daylight (9-11 AM or 4-6 PM)
☑ Clean background (white wall, wooden floor)
☑ Flat lay + hanger shot + detail shots
☑ Measurements (pit-to-pit, length, sleeve)
☑ Flaws photographed honestly
☑ Brand tag visible
☑ Care label visible

**Listing Template That Converts:**

```
Title: Vintage [Brand] [Item Type] [Decade] - [Key Feature]
Example: Vintage 90s Tommy Hilfiger Windbreaker - Oversized Streetwear

Condition: [Excellent/Good/Fair] + note any flaws

Measurements: 
- Pit to Pit: XX inches
- Length: XX inches
- Sleeve: XX inches

Style: [90s / 80s / 70s] vintage [color] [item type]. 
Perfect for [occasion/style]. Made from [material].

Authenticity: 100% genuine vintage piece

Shipping: Ships within 1 business day via [carrier]

Returns: Accepted within 3 days (buyer pays return shipping)

```

---

### Phase 4: Marketing & Growth (Month 2+)

**TikTok Strategy:**
- **3-5 posts/week minimum**
- Content ideas:
  - Thrift flips (before/after)
  - "Find of the week" showcases
  - Pricing breakdowns (what I bought for vs. selling price)
  - Vintage styling tips
  - Packing orders (ASMR style)

**Instagram Strategy:**
- Reels = reach (behind-the-scenes, styling videos)
- Stories = connection (daily polls, Q&A, new arrivals)
- Posts = portfolio (high-quality product shots)
- Use 15-20 relevant hashtags per post

**Email List Building:**
- Every sale = email captured
- Send weekly "New Drop" emails
- Offer 10% off for first purchase
- Build anticipation for restocks

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Not researching prices** – Know what similar items sell for before listing  
❌ **Bad photos** – People buy with their eyes, invest in lighting  
❌ **Vague measurements** – Returns will kill your profits  
❌ **Ignoring platform fees** – Factor in 10-15% platform costs  
❌ **Giving up too soon** – First 50 sales are the hardest
