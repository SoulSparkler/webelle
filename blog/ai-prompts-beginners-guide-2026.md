## How AI Prompts Actually Work

### The Anatomy of a Great Prompt

Every high-performing AI prompt has four elements:

**1. Role** — Tell AI who it is
*"Act as an experienced marketing copywriter..."*

**2. Task** — Be specific about what you want
*"...write a 5-email welcome sequence..."*

**3. Context** — Give it the details it needs
*"...for a virtual assistant service targeting overwhelmed small business owners in Europe..."*

**4. Format** — Tell it how to deliver the output
*"...formatted as separate emails with subject lines, numbered 1-5, each 150-200 words."*

The more specific you are, the more useful the output.

## Section 1: Social Media Prompts (25 Prompts)

### Instagram Captions

```
Act as a social media copywriter specializing in women's entrepreneurship. Write 5 Instagram captions for [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that are conversational, empowering, and end with a call to action. Each caption should be 100-150 words, include 2-3 relevant emojis, and speak to women who want to build income on their own terms. Target audience: women aged 25-45 starting their first business.
```

```
Write 10 Instagram caption hooks (just the opening line) for a [YOUR NICHE] business that would make a woman scroll past and immediately stop. Make them specific, surprising, or challenge a common belief. No generic openings like "Are you tired of..." or "Have you ever wondered..."
```

```
Create a 30-day Instagram content calendar for [YOUR BUSINESS] with: 10 educational posts, 8 personal story posts, 7 client result posts, and 5 promotional posts. For each post, write: the topic, the opening line, and the call to action.
```

### TikTok Scripts

```
Write a 60-second TikTok script for a [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE] that follows this format: hook (first 3 seconds creates curiosity), problem (15 seconds), solution (30 seconds), CTA (12 seconds). The script should be conversational and feel like advice from a friend, not an ad. Tone: confident, warm, real.
```

```
Give me 15 TikTok video ideas for [YOUR BUSINESS] that would get views from women who are looking to start a side business. Focus on content that is educational, behind-the-scenes, or shows a transformation. Include the hook for each video.
```

### LinkedIn Posts

```
Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC RELATED TO YOUR BUSINESS] that positions me as an expert while being authentic and relatable. Length: 200-300 words. Structure: 1 attention-grabbing opening line, 3-5 insight paragraphs with white space between each, CTA at the end. Tone: professional but human.
```

### Pinterest Descriptions

```
Write 10 Pinterest pin descriptions for [YOUR PRODUCT OR SERVICE]. Each description should be 150-200 characters, include primary keyword [YOUR KEYWORD] in the first sentence, be written in second person ("you will learn..."), and include a CTA. Make them feel useful, not salesy.
```

## Section 2: Email Marketing Prompts (20 Prompts)

### Welcome Sequences

```
Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [YOUR BUSINESS]. Email 1: Warm welcome + what to expect. Email 2: Your story + why you started. Email 3: Most valuable free resource or tip. Email 4: Social proof + transformation story. Email 5: Your main offer with soft CTA. Each email should be 150-250 words, conversational tone, with compelling subject lines.
```

```
Write subject lines for 10 welcome emails that would achieve 40%+ open rates for [YOUR AUDIENCE DESCRIPTION]. Make them specific, curiosity-driven, or personal. Avoid spam words. Include a preview text suggestion for each.
```

### Promotional Emails

```
Write a launch email for [YOUR PRODUCT OR SERVICE] priced at [PRICE]. This email should: open with a relatable pain point, present the solution, list 5 key benefits (not features), include social proof, state the price clearly, and have a strong CTA. Length: 300-400 words. Tone: enthusiastic but not pushy.
```

```
Write 3 cart abandonment emails for [YOUR PRODUCT]. Email 1 (1 hour after): gentle reminder with key benefit. Email 2 (24 hours after): address objection + FAQ. Email 3 (48 hours after): last chance + urgency. Each email under 200 words.
```

### Newsletter Content

```
Write a weekly newsletter for [YOUR BUSINESS] subscribers with this structure: 1 personal story or insight (100 words), 3 quick tips related to [YOUR NICHE] (50 words each), 1 resource recommendation with why you love it (50 words), 1 question to prompt replies. Total length: 400-500 words. Friendly, smart tone.
```

## Section 3: Business Writing Prompts (20 Prompts)

### Client Proposals

```
Write a client proposal for [DESCRIBE YOUR SERVICE] for a client who needs [CLIENT PROBLEM]. Structure it as: Executive Summary (what you will deliver), Problem Statement (their current situation), Proposed Solution (your approach), Deliverables (specific outcomes), Timeline (week by week), Investment (use this instead of "price"), and Next Steps. Tone: confident, professional, specific.
```

```
Write 5 different versions of a pricing page for [YOUR SERVICE]: one targeting budget-conscious clients, one targeting premium clients, one focused on results, one focused on time savings, and one focused on peace of mind. Each version 150-200 words.
```

### Client Emails

```
Write a template for following up with a potential client who went quiet after an initial call. The email should: reference our specific conversation, provide one additional value point, address the most common objection for [YOUR SERVICE], and make it easy for them to move forward. Under 150 words. Not desperate or aggressive.
```

```
Write a professional email to a client explaining a delay in delivery for [PROJECT TYPE]. The email should: acknowledge the delay honestly, take responsibility without over-apologizing, give a new realistic timeline, explain what you are doing to ensure quality, and offer one compensation or bonus. Under 200 words.
```

### FAQs and Service Descriptions

```
Write an FAQ page for [YOUR BUSINESS] covering the 10 most common questions from potential clients. Questions should address: pricing, process, timeline, qualifications, what is included, what is not included, payment terms, revisions, communication, and results. Keep each answer under 100 words. Tone: clear, friendly, reassuring.
```


## Section 5: AI Prompt Formulas for Women in Business

### The Transformation Formula

```
Write [CONTENT TYPE] for [TARGET AUDIENCE] who is currently experiencing [CURRENT SITUATION / PAIN] and wants to achieve [DESIRED OUTCOME]. Show them that [YOUR SOLUTION] can get them there by [TIMEFRAME]. Use [TONE] tone. Length: [LENGTH].
```

**Example:**
*Write an Instagram caption for women aged 30-45 who are currently stuck in a 9-5 they hate and want to earn €2,000/month from home by the end of the year. Show them that starting a virtual assistant business can get them there in 90 days. Use motivating, real, and sisterly tone. Length: 120 words.*

### The Problem-Agitate-Solve Formula

```
Using the Problem-Agitate-Solve copywriting framework, write [CONTENT TYPE] for [AUDIENCE]. Problem: [STATE THE PROBLEM]. Agitate: [MAKE THEM FEEL THE PAIN OF NOT SOLVING IT]. Solve: [INTRODUCE YOUR SOLUTION]. End with: [CTA]. Length: [LENGTH]. Tone: [TONE].
```

### The Social Proof Formula

```
Write a [CONTENT TYPE] that features a success story for [CUSTOMER AVATAR] who used [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE] to achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME]. Write it in a storytelling style: setup (who they were before), conflict (what they struggled with), turning point (when they found your solution), resolution (their result), and call to action. Length: [LENGTH].
```

## How to Get the Most Out of These Prompts

### Tip 1: Always Add Your Specifics
The prompts in brackets are placeholders. The more detail you add, the better. Instead of "[TARGET AUDIENCE]" write "women aged 28-42 who are working in marketing but want to start a side business as a virtual assistant."

### Tip 2: Iterate with Follow-Up Prompts
After your first output, use these follow-up prompts to improve it:
- "Make this more conversational and less formal"
- "Add 2 specific examples or statistics"
- "Cut this down to 100 words without losing the key message"
- "Rewrite the opening line to be more attention-grabbing"
- "Change the tone to be warmer and more encouraging"

### Tip 3: Save What Works
When a prompt produces excellent output, save both the prompt AND the output. Build a personal library of your best prompts over time.

### Tip 4: Use AI to Write Better Prompts
Ask ChatGPT: "I want to write an AI prompt for [GOAL]. Help me write the most effective prompt possible by asking me clarifying questions first."

## FAQ

**Q: Which AI tool should I use with these prompts?**
A: Start with ChatGPT (free tier) or Claude (free tier). Both work well. Claude tends to produce longer, more nuanced content while ChatGPT is slightly faster for quick tasks.

**Q: Can I use these prompts for commercial purposes?**
A: Yes. The output from AI tools is yours to use commercially. Always review and edit outputs before publishing.

**Q: How do I make AI sound more like me?**
A: Add this to any prompt: "Write this in my voice: [paste 2-3 examples of your own writing]. Match my tone, sentence length, and personality."

**Q: What if the output is not what I expected?**
A: Try being more specific, add more context, or ask AI to try again with a different angle. You can also ask: "What information do you need from me to write this better?"

**Q: Are there topics AI cannot help with?**
A: AI is not great at highly niche technical topics, recent events (after its training cutoff), or deeply personal advice. For strategy and creativity, it excels.
