## The 5-Platform AI Content Strategy

### Platform 1: Instagram

**The AI workflow for Instagram:**

**Step 1: Content ideation (5 minutes)**
Prompt: *"Generate 20 Instagram content ideas for a [YOUR NICHE] creator targeting [YOUR AUDIENCE]. Mix: educational carousels, relatable memes, personal story Reels, product showcases, and engagement-focused posts. Include the hook for each."*

**Step 2: Caption writing (2 minutes per post)**
Prompt: *"Write an Instagram caption for a post about [TOPIC] for [YOUR AUDIENCE]. Include: attention-grabbing first line, 2-3 sentences of value or story, a call to action (comment/save/share), and 3 relevant hashtags to include. Length: 100-150 words. Brand voice: [DESCRIBE YOUR TONE]."*

**Step 3: Carousel creation (15 minutes)**
- Use Claude to write the text for each slide
- Use Canva AI to design the slides (choose a template, let AI resize and adapt)
- Download and schedule

**Step 4: Reels scripts (5 minutes)**
Prompt: *"Write a 30-second Reels script about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Structure: 3-second hook (creates curiosity without giving it away), 20 seconds of value (3 quick tips or one mini-tutorial), 7-second outro with CTA. Written in a natural, conversational tone — as if talking to a friend."*

**AI-powered Instagram output:** 5 posts per week in 2 hours (versus 10+ hours without AI)

### Platform 2: TikTok

**The AI workflow for TikTok:**

**Step 1: Trend-aware content ideas**
Prompt: *"I create content about [YOUR NICHE] on TikTok. What are 10 TikTok content formats that consistently perform well in my niche that I can adapt? For each format, describe: the structure, why it works psychologically, and give me a specific example I can film today."*

**Step 2: Script writing**
Prompt: *"Write a TikTok script for a 60-second video titled '[VIDEO TOPIC]'. Structure: hook (first 3 seconds — a surprising statement or question), problem setup (10 seconds), solution or value (35 seconds — step by step), punchline or revelation (5 seconds), and CTA (7 seconds). Conversational, punchy, no filler words."*

**Step 3: Captions and hashtags**
Prompt: *"Write 3 versions of TikTok captions for a video about [TOPIC] targeting [AUDIENCE]. Version 1: question-based to drive comments. Version 2: bold statement that creates controversy or curiosity. Version 3: mini-summary that tells viewers exactly what they will learn. Each under 100 characters. Also suggest 5 hashtags."*

**AI-powered TikTok output:** 5 videos per week scripted in under 90 minutes

### Platform 3: YouTube

**The AI workflow for YouTube:**

**Step 1: Video idea validation**
Prompt: *"I create YouTube videos about [YOUR NICHE] for [YOUR AUDIENCE]. My channel is [SMALL/MEDIUM/LARGE]. Give me 10 video title ideas that target high-search keywords, have a strong hook, and position me as an authority. For each title, explain the search intent and what the video should deliver."*

**Step 2: Full video script (10-15 minute video)**
Prompt: *"Write a complete YouTube script for a 12-minute video titled '[VIDEO TITLE].' Include: Hook (30 seconds — why this matters right now), Introduction + why I'm qualified (60 seconds), Main content (10 minutes — 4-5 sections with clear transitions), Summary (30 seconds), CTA (30 seconds — subscribe, comment, watch next). Add [PAUSE] markers where I should stop and add B-roll. Written to sound like I am talking, not reading."*

**Step 3: Thumbnail copy and description**
Prompt: *"Write a YouTube video description for a video about [TOPIC]. Include: 2-sentence hook, 5 bullet points of what viewers will learn, time stamps for key sections (I'll fill in the actual times), links section placeholder, and 3-5 relevant hashtags. Also write 5 thumbnail text options — 2-4 words each, high contrast, creates curiosity."*

**AI-powered YouTube output:** 1-2 fully scripted videos per week in 2 hours of writing time

### Platform 4: Pinterest

**Pinterest is the most underused platform for female creators in 2026 — and AI makes it easy to dominate it.**

**The AI workflow for Pinterest:**

**Step 1: SEO research**
Prompt: *"I create content about [YOUR NICHE] on Pinterest targeting [YOUR AUDIENCE]. What are the 30 highest-traffic keywords I should target? Organize by search volume tier: high (100k+ monthly), medium (10k-100k monthly), and lower but highly targeted (under 10k monthly). Include long-tail keyword variations for each."*

**Step 2: Pin descriptions (batch 20 at a time)**
Prompt: *"Write 20 Pinterest pin descriptions for [YOUR NICHE] content targeting [YOUR AUDIENCE]. Each description should: start with a high-traffic keyword, be 150-200 characters, include a clear benefit statement, use action language, and end with a soft CTA. Keywords to work in: [LIST 5 KEYWORDS]."*

**Step 3: Board optimization**
Prompt: *"I have a Pinterest board called '[BOARD NAME]' about [TOPIC]. Write an optimized board description that: includes primary keyword in first sentence, describes exactly who this board is for, lists 5 types of content they will find, and is 200-250 characters total."*

**AI-powered Pinterest output:** 30 pins described in 30 minutes; consistent SEO across all content

### Platform 5: Email Newsletter

**Your email list is the only platform you truly own. AI makes it easy to be consistent.**

**The AI workflow for your newsletter:**

**Step 1: Monthly planning**
Prompt: *"Plan 4 email newsletters for [YOUR NICHE] audience for the month of [MONTH]. Each newsletter should have: a theme, an attention-grabbing subject line (and 2 alternatives), a preview text, a brief outline of content (200 words), and a CTA. Make them varied: one personal story, one tutorial, one industry insight, one promotional."*

**Step 2: Full newsletter drafts**
Prompt: *"Write a full email newsletter for [YOUR NICHE] subscribers on the topic of '[TOPIC].' Length: 400-600 words. Structure: personal hook or story (100 words), main value content (300 words — practical, specific), quick recommendation or resource (50 words), question to prompt replies (25 words), sign-off. Conversational, direct, smart tone — like an email from a knowledgeable friend."*

## Repurposing: The AI Multiplier Strategy

The biggest time-save in AI content creation is repurposing — taking one piece of content and turning it into many.

**One long-form YouTube video can become:**
- 5 TikTok/Reels clips (use Opus Clip)
- 10 Twitter/X threads (use ChatGPT to extract key points)
- 1 LinkedIn article (use Claude to restructure)
- 1 email newsletter (use AI to summarize and personalize)
- 5 Pinterest pin ideas (use AI to extract visual tips)
- 1 Instagram carousel (use Canva AI to design from bullet points)
- 1 blog post (use Claude to expand to 1,500 words)

**Prompt for repurposing:**
```
Here is the transcript from my YouTube video: [PASTE TRANSCRIPT]. 

Create the following from this transcript:
1. 5 TikTok scripts (60 seconds each, different angles)
2. 5 Instagram caption ideas with hooks
3. A 10-tweet thread with the key insights
4. A 600-word email newsletter on the topic
5. A 1,200-word blog post with SEO structure
6. 5 Pinterest pin descriptions

Keep my brand voice throughout: [DESCRIBE YOUR VOICE].
```

This single prompt takes a 30-minute video and produces 6 weeks of cross-platform content in 15 minutes.

## FAQ

**Q: Is it dishonest to use AI to create content?**
A: No more than using any other tool. Your ideas, perspective, and expertise are what make the content valuable. AI helps you express and distribute those ideas more efficiently. Audiences respond to quality and consistency — AI helps you deliver both.

**Q: Will my audience notice that I use AI?**
A: Only if the content lacks your authentic voice. Always edit AI output to sound like you. Add your personal stories, your specific examples, your opinions. AI provides the structure; you provide the personality.

**Q: Can AI help me grow faster on a new account?**
A: Yes — especially for consistency and SEO. AI helps you post regularly when motivation is low and optimize every piece of content for discoverability. These are the two biggest growth factors for new accounts.

**Q: Which platform should I start with?**
A: If you prefer writing, start with LinkedIn or newsletter. If you prefer video, start with TikTok or YouTube Shorts. Pinterest is worth adding for any creator as a long-term traffic driver.

**Q: Can small creators (under 1,000 followers) use this strategy?**
A: Absolutely. AI is especially valuable early in your creator journey because it helps you find your voice faster and build a consistent presence before your audience is large enough to sustain you.
