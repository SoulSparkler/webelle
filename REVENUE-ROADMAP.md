# WebElle Revenue Roadmap — $500/Month Goal

> **Status: UPDATED Aug 2026.** Supersedes the earlier Etsy volume strategy. WebElle now sells premium digital kits directly from webelle.store.

## Target Breakdown
- **Monthly Goal:** $500 USD
- **Primary Product:** Business Starter Kits sold direct (webelle.store)
- **Pricing (USD only):**
  - Starter Kit: **$47**
  - Premium (Kit + Website): **$97**
  - Business in a Box template library: **$9.99**
- **Required Sales (blended):** ~6 Starter sales/month → $300, or 4 Premium → $388, or a realistic mix of Starter + premium + the $9.99 add-on
- **Strategy:** QUALITY over volume. Fewer, higher-ticket sales, no marketplace fee, full margin on every order.

## Where We Sell
- **webelle.store (Stripe checkout)** — primary storefront. Self-hosted, no per-sale marketplace cut.
- **Gumroad** — candidate secondary/launch channel (Week 2 decision pending).
- No Etsy dependency. A marketplace that blocked us once should not power our core loop.
